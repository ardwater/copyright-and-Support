Help (tap a question to open it)

How do I add photos and videos?
Tap Add and choose any mix of photos and videos. You can add more at any time. To reorder, open Clips, press and hold a clip and drag it. Select a clip and tap Delete to remove it.

How do I play only part of a video?
Open Clips, select the video, tap Trim and drag the handles at either end of the filmstrip. Tap ✕ to cancel, ✓ to keep, or Reset to play the whole video again.

How do I split, duplicate or freeze a clip?
Open Clips and drag the line above the panel to put the playhead where you want. Tap Split to cut the clip in two there, Freeze to turn that frame into a still, or Duplicate to copy the selected clip. Speed (0.3× to 3×) and each clip’s own Volume are in the same row.

How do I add music or a voiceover?
Open the Audio tab. Tap Add music to pick a file from Files, then set its volume and where in the song it starts. Tap Voiceover to record over the video, and Stop when you’re done. You can also change the level of the videos’ own sound there.

How do I add text and stickers?
Open the Text tab (or tap Text on the main bar). Tap Add text, type, and pick a font, style and colour. Tap Stickers for emoji and ready-made labels like #hashtag, @mention, time and date. Drag to move, pinch to resize and twist with two fingers to rotate. Drag an item to the bin that appears to delete it, or tap a selected text again to edit it. Under Starts and Ends you can choose when it appears. Text and stickers are free.

How does the finger-frame camera work?
Tap Edit on the main bar, then Camera (the first tool). Make an L with both thumbs and index fingers: the four fingertips become the corners of a frame, and your clips (photos and videos, in order) stretch across it (or a soft colour gradient if you have not added any), so pulling your hands apart stretches the picture too. Your hands stay in front of it. With no hands in view, the frame sits across your eyes. Choose Auto, Hands or Face, then tap the red button to record. The camera is vertical, and the recording is added to your clips. Recording is a Pro feature.

What are FRONT and TOON in the camera?
FRONT keeps your hands and forearms in front of the frame, as if you were holding it. TOON gives your face and hands a flat, cartoon-style colour. Both can be switched off if your phone gets warm.

The frame jumps or doesn’t follow my hands.
Use bright, even light and keep both hands fully in view with the thumb and index finger spread. Keep your hands out of heavy shadow and avoid busy skin-coloured backgrounds. If the phone is warm, the effect lowers its work automatically. Triple-tap the status pill at the top of the camera to see live numbers you can send us with a bug report.

How do I change the P, S and T letters?
Open Edit, scroll to P S T, and type up to one character in each box. Leave a box empty to hide that letter. This is a Pro feature.

How do the edit tools work?
Open Edit, pick a tool and slide the ruler. Tap the number’s ↺ button to reset that tool, ✕ to undo your changes, or ✓ to keep them. A dot under a tool means it has been changed. Under Looks you can try a ready-made style with one tap.

Can I undo a mistake?
Yes. The two arrows at the top left undo and redo your changes, including deleting clips. Your project is also saved automatically as a draft, so it is still there when you reopen Postgam.

Can I export for Instagram feed posts?
Yes. In Export, choose 9:16 Reel, 4:5 Feed or 1:1 Square under Format.

The Save Video button doesn’t work.
Postgam needs permission to add to your Photos library. Open the Settings app → Postgam → Photos and allow access, then try again. You can also use Share to send the file to Files or another app.

Voiceover doesn’t record.
Postgam needs microphone access. Open the Settings app → Postgam → Microphone and allow it, then try again. Headphones give the cleanest recording.

Export is slow or fails.
Higher resolutions, 60 fps and long videos take longer and need free storage. Try 1080 wide at 30 fps, make sure you have free space, and keep Postgam open until rendering finishes.

I bought Pro but it’s locked.
Tap Restore purchases below while signed in with the Apple ID you used to buy it. Pro is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple ID.

Does Postgam add a watermark?
Free exports include a small “Postgam” tag near the bottom. Postgam Pro is a one-time purchase that removes it from every export and unlocks all controls.

The watermark is still on my video.
Videos you rendered before unlocking Pro keep their “Postgam” tag. Tap Restore purchases below if Pro shows as locked, check that Export → Output → Watermark says None, then render the video again.

There is also a Show intro again button at the end of the Help list.
