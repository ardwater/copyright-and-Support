Help

Each question below opens to show its answer.

How do I add photos and videos?
Tap Add and choose any mix of photos and videos. You can add more at any time, then reorder or delete them under Media → Order.

How do I play only part of a video?
Videos play in full by default. Open Media → Timing → Video length and drag Start and End. Tap Play full video to reset.

The Save Video button doesn’t work.
POST needs permission to add to your Photos library. Open the Settings app → POST → Photos and allow access, then try again. You can also use Share to send the file to Files or another app.

Export is slow or fails.
Higher resolutions, 60 fps and long videos take longer and need free storage. Try 1080 × 1920 at 30 fps, make sure you have free space, and keep POST open until rendering finishes.

I bought Pro but it’s locked.
Tap Restore purchases below while signed in with the Apple ID you used to buy it. Pro is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple ID.

Does POST add a watermark?
Free exports include a small “Made with POST” tag near the bottom. POST Pro is a one-time purchase that removes it from every export and unlocks all controls.

The watermark is still on my video.
Videos you rendered before unlocking Pro keep their tag. Tap Restore purchases below if Pro shows as locked, check that Export → Output → Watermark says None, then render the video again.

There is also a Show intro again button at the bottom of this list, which replays the onboarding screens.
